Iran state TV reportedly said that a potential deal with US would reopen shipping through the
[Strait of Hormuz](https://economictimes.indiatimes.com/web/20260528032735/https://economictimes.indiatimes.com/topic/strait-of-hormuz).
Brent North Sea crude, the international benchmark, fell five percent to $94.61 a barrel, while the main US contract, WTI, tumbled 5.9 percent to $88.31 a barrel.
