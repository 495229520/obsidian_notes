Shares of Cyient dropped nearly 6% on Wednesday as the stock turned ex-record date for its Rs 720 crore share buyback, priced at Rs 1,125 per share, which implies a premium of about 24% over the previous closing price.
The engineering and technology services firm had set June 17 (Wednesday) as the record date for the buyback. Only shareholders holding the stock in their demat accounts as of this date will be eligible to tender shares. Investors initiating fresh positions will receive shares under SEBI’s T+1 settlement cycle on the next day, making them ineligible to participate in the buyback.

Sectoral breadth remained positive with most indices trading in the green, signalling broad-based buying interest. Nifty Consumer Durables led gains with nearly 2% upside, followed by IT and Media, indicating strength in consumption and technology themes. Metal, Realty and PSU Bank remained under pressure, suggesting selective profit booking despite an overall constructive market tone.
- Nifty bias remains positive and is currently placed around the upper band of the last two months falling channel which also coincides with the previous swing high of 26th May placed around the range of 24,050 - 24,100.
- Going ahead, a move above 24,100 will infuse further momentum and open upside towards 24,600 levels in the coming weeks.
- Failure to do so will lead to some consolidation in the range of 23,600 - 24,100 in the coming sessions.
- We believe that the overall structure is positive, and any dips should be used to accumulate quality stocks in a staggered manner. We expect the index to eventually breakout above the 24,100 levels and gradually head towards 24,600 levels.
- Immediate support is placed at 23,800 levels. While key short-term support is revised higher towards 23,500 - 23,600 levels being the confluence of the 20 days EMA and the recent breakout area.
