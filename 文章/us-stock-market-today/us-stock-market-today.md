# US Stock Market Today

![image](images/img_000_d5224695.jpg)

LIVE BLOG
Wall St futures steady as Dell jumps, Gap drops; US-Iran deal watch
Synopsis
Dow Jones Today | US Stock Market Live Updates: US stock futures were steady near record highs as investors tracked US-Iran ceasefire extension talks and Strait of Hormuz shipping outlook. Dell surged on raised guidance while Gap slumped after cutting forecasts. Tech earnings and Fed rate path remain in focus.
Dow Jones Today | Nasdaq | S&P 500 | US Stock Market Live Updates: US stock index futures were largely unchanged after recent record highs as markets weighed geopolitical developments involving a possible US-Iran ceasefire extension and easing tensions in the Strait of Hormuz. Dell surged sharply after lifting its full-year outlook, boosting sentiment across AI-linked hardware stocks. In contrast, Gap fell after cutting its sales forecast. Investors also digested mixed US data showing sticky inflation and softer GDP, while awaiting Fed policy cues.
Show more1 New Update
Investors digested fresh US data showing inflation rising at its fastest pace in three years alongside a downward revision in Q1 GDP growth. Despite mixed signals, markets still expect the Federal Reserve to hold rates steady for much of the year, though officials remain cautious about persistent inflation risks. Attention now turns to upcoming Fed commentary for policy direction clues.
Market action remained stock-specific as Gap plunged after cutting its annual sales forecast, signaling pressure on consumer spending. In contrast, Okta rose after reporting stronger-than-expected revenue, while International Flavors & Fragrances gained on reports of a potential multi-billion-dollar asset sale. The divergence highlighted selective strength in corporate performance across sectors.
Dell surged sharply after raising its full-year profit and revenue forecast, reinforcing optimism around AI-driven infrastructure demand. The rally spilled over into related hardware names, with peers such as Hewlett Packard Enterprise and Super Micro Computer also gaining. The move strengthened the broader narrative of sustained AI investment supporting tech sector earnings growth.
Markets closely tracked reports that the US and Iran had agreed to extend a ceasefire and potentially ease shipping restrictions through the Strait of Hormuz. However, uncertainty remained as final approval from US leadership was still pending. The development was seen as a key factor for oil prices, global trade flows, and broader risk sentiment across equity markets.
US stock index futures were largely flat after the previous session’s record closes, reflecting a pause in momentum as investors weighed geopolitical signals and earnings updates. Sentiment remained cautiously positive, with markets still supported by AI-led gains and expectations of resilient corporate earnings. Traders stayed focused on macro cues and geopolitical headlines that could shift risk appetite in the near term.
