# Carnival Time! Auto cos on a roll as demand stays strong

![image](images/img_000_0d104658.jpg)

![Carnival Time! Auto cos on a roll as demand stays strong](https://img.etimg.com/thumb/msid-131754020,width-300,height-225,imgsize-29074,resizemode-75/carnival-time-auto-cos-on-a-roll-as-demand-stays-strong.jpg)
As per data available with industry body Society of Indian Automobile Manufacturers (SIAM), sales of passenger vehicles rose 27% from a year earlier to 438,854 units in May.
[Two-wheeler sales](https://economictimes.indiatimes.com/topic/two-wheeler-sales)increased 31%, while three-wheelers volume grew 14.8%. These are dispatches from manufacturers to dealerships and not retail sales.
"Passenger vehicles, three-wheelers and two-wheelers recorded the highest ever sales of May in 2026," SIAM director-general Rajesh Menon said. He cited a favourable base effect due to weak sales a year earlier, GST rate cuts implemented last September and easier financing options for the strong May sales.
![Carnival Time! Auto cos on a roll as demand stays strong Carnival Time! Auto cos on a roll as demand stays strong](https://img.etimg.com/photo/msid-42031747/et-logo.jpg)
Demand was strong for hatchbacks, sedans and SUVs, senior industry executives said. At
[Maruti Suzuki](https://economictimes.indiatimes.com/maruti-suzuki-india-ltd/stocks/companyid-11890.cms), sales expanded across segments, said Partho Banerjee, senior executive officer (marketing and sales). Sales of mini cars Alto and S-Presso rose nearly two-and-a-half times to 16,275 units from 6,776 units a year earlier. Utility vehicle sales grew 44% to 79,267 units.
In the two-wheeler segment, sales of scooters rose 27% to 739,667 units. Motorcycle sales increased 7% to 1,113,973 units on a high base.
